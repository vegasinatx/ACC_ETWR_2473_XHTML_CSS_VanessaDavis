Hello and welcome!

If you’ve somehow stumbled across this little corner of the internet, here’s a bit about me. I was born and raised in Austin before it became the cool "it" city. Around here, that makes me something of a unicorn. I went to a small liberal arts school in California called Chapman University and majored in Film… also before it became cool. I’m starting to notice a pattern here.

After graduating, I joined Technicolor Sound Services... yes, that Technicolor you see in the old movie credits. I didn’t work in color, but in sound. From 2007 to 2023, if you watched a Paramount movie in a theater, on a plane, or streaming from your couch, chances are I had a hand in the audio you heard.

The film and tv industry strikes in 2023 gave me the nudge to explore new creative directions. Since then, I’ve shifted into podcasting and helping small businesses grow their digital presence through podcast production, social media strategy, and workflow management. I also hold an MBA in Digital Media Management from St. Edward’s University and love building customized databases and project systems using Airtable or similar platforms.

When I’m not behind a computer, you’ll probably find me on the road somewhere. I’m a big fan of solo travel and document my adventures on my blog, Gastank Chronicles where I share stories about “traveling local and far, one gas tank at a time.”

Outside of work, my life is "ruled" by two dogs. Winston, a gentleman and professional napper since birth, and Tibbee, his daughter, a classic Schnauzer: bossy, sweet, and endlessly loyal once she’s done barking her introduction. Their photos are below if you need a quick serotonin boost.

Apparently, I also have a habit of saying “I also have…” quite a lot. So there’s that too.
